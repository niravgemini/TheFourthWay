The workflow

Four steps.
Every decision visible.

Explore how a proposed change moves from account data to an approved action.

Step 01 / Read

Understand the account.

Review campaign settings, account details, performance metrics, and search terms from Google Ads accounts the operator is authorized to manage.

Reading data does not change campaigns.

Step 02 / Plan

See the proposed change.

Prepare a campaign or a budget or status update. The plan brings together the proposed operations, account context, policy checks, and Google validation.

Changes are described before they are executed.

Step 03 / Review

A human makes the decision.

Nirav reviews the exact plan in a separate approval interface. He can approve it or reject it. Approval records the decision; execution is a separate step.

Approval itself does not change Google Ads.

Step 04 / Apply

Execute the approved plan.

After approval, the tool checks the account, permissions, current state, and write settings again. A changed or expired plan needs a fresh review.

New campaigns are created paused.

A few useful answers

Before
we begin.

Can I sign in and use the tool myself?

The current version is privately operated by Nirav for Fourthway Labs and authorized client work. It does not offer public self-service registration. Contact Nirav to discuss your account or requirements.

Will the AI change campaigns automatically?

The intended workflow requires a human to approve the exact plan before execution. AI integration can help read data and prepare plans. The current release assumes trusted local software; it is not an isolation boundary against software with unrestricted access to the same computer.

Is production Google Ads access already approved?

Production API access is pending. The tool has been developed and checked offline, and integration work is ongoing. This page does not claim Google approval or that a production integration is already available.

Is Fourthway Labs affiliated with Google?

Fourthway Labs is an independent sole proprietorship owned and operated by Nirav Bhatia. Fourthway Ads Agent is an independent tool designed to integrate with the Google Ads API.

How can account access be removed?

Contact Nirav to stop processing and address stored-data deletion. Google access can also be revoked through your Google Account connections, and manager or user access can be removed in Google Ads. Revoking access does not automatically remove previously stored records. See the privacy page for details.
