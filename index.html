02 / The fourth way

See the whole.
Build with intention.

Design gives an idea its form. Technology gives it structure. Intelligence makes it useful. The fourth way is bringing them together with purpose.

I.

II.

Make the useful thing.

Choose the right tools, shape a simple solution, and keep the important decisions visible.

III.

Test, learn, refine.

Check how it works in practice. Improve the details that make the everyday experience better.
